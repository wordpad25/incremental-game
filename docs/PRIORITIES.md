# PRIORITIES

The following initiatives represent the next development cycle for "Incremental Knowledge", focusing on visual evolution, world-building, and consistency.

### 1. Thematic Eras & Visual Evolution
* **Vector:** Content Depth / Visual Feedback
* **Scope:** Medium
* **Description:** Break the game progression into "Eras" (e.g., Neolithic, Industrial, Digital, Galactic). Each era changes the background theme, font, and generator icons. Advancing an era requires reaching a Fragment/sec threshold AND completing a "Milestone Exam" (e.g., 25 reviews in one session). This adds a strong visual reward for long-term progression.

### 2. Research Expeditions (Map System)
* **Vector:** SRS Integration / Content Depth
* **Scope:** Large
* **Description:** Add a second tab/view for "Expeditions". Players spend Researchers to start an expedition on a map. Progress is moved by card reviews (1 card = 1 step). Reaching waypoints grants unique "Artifacts" (permanent buffs like +15% Gem chance). This transforms cards into literal fuel for exploration.

### 3. Study Streaks & Mastery Combos
* **Vector:** SRS Integration Quality
* **Scope:** Small
* **Description:** Reward long-term habits. A daily study streak (tracked in state) provides a cumulative production bonus (+5%/day, max 50%). Additionally, getting 5 "Easy" ratings in a row triggers a "Focus Burst" (30 seconds of doubled production). This rewards both consistency and recall quality.

### 4. Knowledge Tree Visualization
* **Vector:** Visual Feedback / Milestone Tracking
* **Scope:** Medium
* **Description:** Replace the static stats panel with a "Knowledge Tree". As players hit review milestones, the tree grows branches representing different subjects or tiers of knowledge. Hovering over branches shows the specific study achievements that unlocked them.
