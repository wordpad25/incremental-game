# Brainstorm: Expanding Playthrough & Balancing Pacing

To expand the game's lifespan from a week-long experience to a months-long study companion, we need to address pacing, soft caps, and endless sinks for our existing features.

## 1. The Neural Map: Intermediate Synergies & Soft Caps
**The Problem**: Jumping from a 10K cost generator to a 1M cost generator creates a "dead zone" where the player just waits for days doing nothing.
**The Expansion**:
*   **Support Nodes (Horizontal Expansion)**: Add supplementary nodes branching off the main generators (e.g., "Cooling Systems" off Datacenter, "Ethics Protocol" off Quantum AI). These cost intermediate amounts (e.g., 50K) and provide multiplicative synergies to ONLY their parent node.
*   **Pacing Mechanism**: Introduce a "Soft Cap" penalty on generator costs. After buying 25 of the same generator, the cost exponent jumps from `1.15` to `1.50`, forcing the player to expand their tree rather than endlessly farming cheap nodes.

## 2. Thought Construct: Evolution & Prestige Scaling
**The Problem**: Once the construct is "Sated", there is no further goal; it's a binary state.
**The Expansion**:
*   **Construct Eras / Evolution**: The construct earns 1 "Growth Point" for every consecutive day it remains Sated. At 7, 30, and 90 points, it evolves (Wisp -> Orb -> Entity -> Avatar).
*   **Balance mechanism**: Each evolution permanently lowers the decay rate of Frenzy and permanently increases base click power, providing a months-long pacing goal to maintain daily study habits.

## 3. Active Expeditions: Relic Synthesis (Endgame Sink)
**The Problem**: Eventually, active players will earn all 4 Relics. After that, there is no reason to do Active Expeditions other than fragment bursts.
**The Expansion**:
*   **Relic Synthesis Forge**: Introduce a mechanic where duplicate Relics can be fused.
*   **Balance/Pacing**: Combining 3 Level 1 "Rosetta Stones" plus 100 *Focus Shards* will forge a Level 2 "Rosetta Stone" (increasing the buff slightly). This creates an endless sink for Rating-Specific Resources (Sparks/Shards/Cores) and gives players a reason to infinitely grind Expeditions during their study sessions.

## 4. Rating-Specific Sinks: The "Memory Market"
**The Problem**: If a player never buys the premium upgrades, Sparks, Shards, and Cores will bloat infinitely.
**The Expansion**:
*   **Consumables**: Add temporary, 1-hour buffs that cost these sub-currencies. 
    *   *Examples*: Spend 50 Resilience Cores for a "Streak Shield" (prevents starvations/breakages for 24h). Spend 20 Clarity Sparks for a "Fragment Surge" (10x production for 5 minutes). These act as perfect sinks to keep the economy balanced long-term.
